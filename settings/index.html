To get a refresh token:

  1. Log in to tools.developer.homey.app with your Athom account.
  2. Open your browser's developer tools (F12) and go to the Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox) tab.
  3. Under Local Storage, find the entry for tools.developer.homey.app and copy the value of refreshToken.
  4. Paste it above and press Save.

Need help finding your refresh token? View the documentation.
